Design ONE clock face with the tft-clock skill, in
/Users/bilibili/coding/lcd_clock/.claude/skills/tft-clock (run every command
from that directory). Key: 'constructivist'.

The display: a 0.96" IPS colour LCD, 160 x 80 px, RGB565, 60 fps possible.

THE OWNER'S REQUEST, VERBATIM: "it seems the recently created faces are all
in very similar style, could you create 8 more with different styles? no
need to follow any requirements I or previous design prompts give, just
unleash all your creativity and make amazing faces, feel free to be
adventurous."

What they mean by "similar": the last 12 faces all followed one formula:
four numerals made of a theme's material standing in a row, flat fills with
a dark keyline, a squash on a beat every second, flourishes at :15/:30/:45,
the material reshaping itself at the minute. Break that formula. You decide
everything: the visual style and rendering technique, the composition, the
camera, the rhythm, the palette, whether the time is digits, words, hands,
positions or something nobody has done. Be bold; a safe, pretty face is the
failure here. It is still a clock, so someone looking at it should be able
to find out the time, however unusual the way.

Your starting spark (take it, twist it, or throw it away for something
better):
Russian avant-garde: Rodchenko, El Lissitzky, Stepanova. A kinetic propaganda poster of wedges, bars, circles and diagonals in red, black and cream, with bold slab type and halftone photomontage.

The other seven agents this round: droste, lowpoly, lemmings, turing, orrery, magritte, desktop. Don't converge on their
territory.
