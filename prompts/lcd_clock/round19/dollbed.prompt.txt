puppy and a shaggy panda. This face stars them. The whole point is that the
owner looks at it and recognises THEIR two toys, so likeness matters as much
as the idea: get the silhouettes, proportions, colour blocks and fur right,
and draw at least one of them big enough that those traits read on the glass
(roughly 30+ px tall). They are soft toys with personality, not real
animals. Make them lovable and give them a little life of their own.
