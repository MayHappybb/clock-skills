Design ONE clock face for a monochrome 128x64 (0.96") OLED with the oled-clock skill
(.claude/skills/oled-clock). Read SKILL.md and references/gotchas.md first.
Edit only sim/designs/ants-ds.js (already registered as a placeholder; give it a
real name and blurb). Don't touch other designs, index.js or sim/js/, and don't
render other designs.

Starting idea (push past it, or replace it if you find something stronger):
An ant colony: ants carry crumbs and lay pheromone trails, and the colony builds and rebuilds the time.

GOAL: a stranger looks at it and says "wait, that's a clock?" Creativity comes
first. A competent, pretty, conventional face is a fail. The bar to beat is
scope, cube, numberfolk, nixie and urwerk.

REQUIREMENTS
1. Concept: the time is expressed by the theme's own mechanism. Test: if
   swapping your digits for a plain bitmap font still works, the digits are not
   part of the idea yet.
2. Letterforms: draw your own, made of or carried by the theme's material. No
   kit fonts (font5x7, font3x5, strokeDigit, segDigit, dotText) for the time.
   Design all ten digits as a set. Any date or label speaks the same visual
   language, or leave it out.
3. Layout: not four big centred digits with decoration around them. Use a
   scene, depth, a dial, a mechanism, hours and minutes as different systems.
4. Always alive: smooth 60 fps sub-pixel motion that belongs to the theme,
   every second, not only at the turn.
5. Minute turn: the old digit physically becomes the new one through the
   theme's process, 0.8-3 s, eased, no pops or cuts. Never show a wrong time.
   The hour gets a bigger, distinct show.
6. Composition: one hero, one or two supporting elements, a designed
   background, plenty of black. It must read at 1x: no tiny glyphs, no noise
   as texture. Every element belongs to the same world.
7. Characters, if any, are carefully hand-drawn (sprites beat formulas at this
   size) and interact with the time.
8. Technical: frame() is a pure function of time; `node tools/check.mjs
   --design ants-ds` passes; frame() under ~2 ms. No hidden extra features.

PROCESS
1. Pitch three very different concepts, none like an existing design
   (`node tools/render.mjs --list`). Pick the strongest, judging harshly.
2. Digits first: render out/ants-ds-digits.png and iterate until every digit is
   beautiful on its own.
3. Build, then render out/ants-ds.png, out/ants-ds-turn.png (--sheet across
   10:09:58), out/ants-ds-hour.png (--sheet across 10:59:58), out/ants-ds.gif and
   out/ants-ds-hour.gif. Look at every image.
4. Ask: is the font, layout, motion or transition ordinary? Is it fascinating?
   Fix the weakest part and re-render. At least two rounds.
5. Report: the idea in one sentence, render paths, the check.mjs result, and
   the honest weakest point.
